When exercising, the idea is that you start with the Warmup and work your way down through the exercises.

How to use the app

When you are done with an exercise, click the small done button in the left bottom corner to dismiss the exercise and move on to the next one.
If you want to do an exercise later, just click do later and we will push it down in the queue.
